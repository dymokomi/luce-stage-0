Loops and ranges

range(low, high) counts up, excluding the high end.

for x in collection walks a list, a rank-1 array, or a map's keys. The two-name form binds a position and then a payload — the index and the element for a sequence, the key and the value for a map.

while takes a bool, and break and continue do what you expect. Both of them free whatever the scopes they leave still own.

Nested loops are ordinary loops. There are no labelled breaks; a flag or an early return covers it.
